Iffah Shield: a free content blocker for Android

A free, open-source Android app that helps you lower your gaze and protect your heart and your family. No ads, no subscription, no internet.

⬇️ Download free

🛡️ What it blocks

  • New: on-device AI image detection 👁 It looks at the screen itself and blocks indecent images and videos even with no words in them, such as AI-generated ones. Everything runs on the phone.
  • Inappropriate sites and words in every app and browser: 1000+ built-in terms in 25+ languages.
  • Forced safe search on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and Yahoo.
  • Instagram and Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.
  • Incognito tabs, inappropriate notifications, and any app you choose.

🔒 Holds in a weak moment

  • Strict mode: turning protection off needs a 24-hour wait.
  • Uninstall protection, and protection for DNS and VPN settings.
  • "I feel weak now" button: breathing, your reasons, call a friend, full lockdown.

🌱 Helps you recover

  • Morning and evening adhkar with a tasbih-style counter.
  • A recovery plan based on behavioural therapy and Islamic guidance, a daily self-check, and day medals.
  • Night lock, and a kids mode for parents.
